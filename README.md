# WarioWaree


This game is a duck-themed collection of short, arcade-style minigames made with Godot for the Stardance mission. You play as a little duck and take on quick challenges, including dodging falling stars and collecting items.

The goal is to make it through each challenge, keep your chances, and reach the end of the game.

Controls

• Left and Right Arrow: Move • Space or Up Arrow: Jump where jumping is available

Controls can vary between minigames.
